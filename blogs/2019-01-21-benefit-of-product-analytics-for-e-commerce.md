As the race to acquire more market share intensifies e-commerce companies are doing everything they can to retain their current customers and gain new ones. This fierce competition led e-commerce companies to invest more into analytics platforms. E-commerce companies face several issues in acquiring and retaining new customers while not burning a hole in their pocket.

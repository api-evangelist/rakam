From a small startup to a big enterprise company, people care about your ideas only if you can back them with data. If you’re a marketing person who wants to create marketing campaign that requires you to have enough budget, you probably need to have a hypothesis backed by the data. If you’re a product person, you either ask your users or collect their data in order to be able to decide the features that your developer colleagues will be implementing.

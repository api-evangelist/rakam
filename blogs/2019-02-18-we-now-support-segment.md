We have been working hard to complete the Segment integration for our current and prospective customers, and now we are happy to announce that our integration with Segment is ready to rock n’ roll. Segment is a great tool that allows you to gather your all company data into your data warehouse. You can connect your Facebook and Google account, integrate their SDK into your product, enable the third-party tools that you use for different purposes such as CRM, Hiring, and Payment and let Segment collect all the data into your database.

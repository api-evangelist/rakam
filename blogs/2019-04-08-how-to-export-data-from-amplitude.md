Amplitude is a wonderful tool for non-technical people to analyze the customer event data but as your team expands your needs also evolve so you might need SQL access to your customer event data for data analysts & scientists. Majority of the analytics services won’t support SQL and asks you to pay a hefty fee for external data integration. If you have a deep pocket, you can export the raw event data after you pay the fee.

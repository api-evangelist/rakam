Meet rakam’s newest product, metriql: The first open-source metrics store where companies can define their metrics centrally as code on top of their dbt projects and then sync their data models to multiple BI or data tools at once. metriql was born as a spin-off project from rakam as we decided to open-source rakam’s modeling layer and integrate it with other BI and data tools so companies using rakam for product analytics can also make use of their data models inside other BI and data tools for further analysis.

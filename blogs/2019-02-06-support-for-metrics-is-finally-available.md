We have been working hard in order to make our user interface useful and simple. Even though we have taxonomy feature which allows you to add labels, categories and descriptions to your tables and views but for non-technical people, it’s still a bit blurry to analyze metrics such as revenue, unique users or sessions because every time they use our Segmentation feature, they need to define them again and again.

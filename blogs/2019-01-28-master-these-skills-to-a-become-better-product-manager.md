We did some research on what makes a good product manager and gathered some intel from the various sources such as books, articles, and interviews. We put together a list about the common traits of the best product managers but left out some of the typical things like “become a good person”, “work hard”, “be respectful” and so on. Before starting, we want to say that, you might not have all of the skills listed here and it’s normal, you can’t have everything, but you have to focus on the skills you have, focus on your strengths and become even better.

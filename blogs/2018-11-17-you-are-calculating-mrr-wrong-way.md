From the conversations I had with the startups around me, I have realised that many of them do know about the important metrics such as LTV, CAC and MRR. But they are not interpreting these metrics correctly and this can lead to misleading information about your metrics. In this article, I will write about some of the mistakes I have seen when calculating MRR.
